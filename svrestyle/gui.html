Card Restyle

Cards

Every card you load or add joins this list; × takes one out (with its edits). The list and your edits save themselves after every change (as a dated project until you name it, top right) and open on their own next time.

Older cards are restyled; Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution cards keep their look and only what you edit is set again. Older Trainers become Scarlet & Violet Trainers; Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution Trainers keep their art and their text is set again. Energy is listed but skipped. Edits you make to a card are kept for the zip; with holofoil on, the zip also holds each card's foil textures.

Card

Regular Pokémon (one Prize) can change type: the frame, the name-bar icon and the text colour follow. Attack costs, weakness and resistance stay as they are; change them too if the new type needs it.

One paragraph per line. The Trainer type's rule is printed by the frame, so leave it out of the text. {G} {R} {W} {L} {P} {F} {D} {M} {N} {C} print as energy symbols. HP: only fossils and dolls played as Pokémon (they print it at the right of the name and get their Ability under the text); an attack makes the card a Technical Machine.

The portrait is the pre-evolution's picture from a Scarlet & Violet or Mega Evolution card that evolves from it. Name a card id to choose: a Stage card gives its portrait, any other card the middle of its art.

Abilities

Attacks

Click an energy below an attack to add it to the cost; click a cost symbol to remove it. Damage: a number, optionally followed by +, × or −. In text, {G} {R} {W} {L} {P} {F} {D} {M} {N} {C} print as energy symbols.

Weakness, resistance, retreat

Click a retreat symbol to remove it. With holofoil on, the foil plates follow these icons.

Holofoil

Holofoil needs a Pokémon TCG Live extract: start the GUI with --tcglive DIR.

The white plate is where the foil shows (white) and where not (black): the art keeps the old card's plate with the Pokémon left out; the frame is the new era's, with what this card prints cut out. Move the pointer over the card to tilt the light.

Render

Load a card by its TCGdex id, or import a custom-set export.

Pokémon V become Scarlet & Violet ex cards; regular Sun & Moon and Sword & Shield Pokémon become Mega Evolution cards. Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution cards are edited in place. Older Trainers (Items, Supporters, Stadiums, Pokémon Tools) become Scarlet & Violet Trainers.
